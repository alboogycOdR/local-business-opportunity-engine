# Sprint 8 — Offer and Outreach Drafting

Sprint 8 creates deterministic, operator-reviewable outreach draft packages
for businesses with an approved concept demo. Drafts are not sends: no email,
WhatsApp, SMS, CRM, consent, or contacted event is created.

Eligibility requires `APPROVED_FOR_OUTREACH`, an approved demo with passed
QA and a successful human approval review, a current Business Brief, no
suppression or policy hold, and supported draft channels. Excentric-style
`score_only` businesses have no approved demo and remain ineligible.

Supported offers map directly from the approved demo type: starter website,
website refresh, conversion upgrade, or technical cleanup. Each package can
contain email, WhatsApp, phone-script, and operator manual-note drafts. Copy
uses verified business identity and neutral evidence-backed opportunity
language. It includes the independent concept disclaimer and asks permission
before any future details are sent.

Safety checks block or flag official-site claims, fake prices or testimonials,
unsupported awards, deceptive urgency, insulting language, unsupported
opportunities, suppression, ambiguity, and overuse of personal data. Drafts
remain `ready` for operator review, and the business remains
`APPROVED_FOR_OUTREACH`; Sprint 9 can add consent and outreach-ready controls.
