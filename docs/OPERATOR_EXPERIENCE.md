# Operator experience

Start at `/ui`. The dashboard links to campaigns, queues, pilots, reports, admin, and system status. Lead pages show lifecycle state, score/action, evidence, demo/outreach/CRM context, proposal and delivery links. Queues explain why work is blocked and provide safe next actions.

LBOE never sends email, WhatsApp, SMS, or CRM messages. Manual communication occurs outside LBOE and is recorded as an operator assertion.
# Operator Experience

The operator's primary question is: **where is the next best opportunity, why does it matter, and what should I do next?**

## Opportunity Cards

The `/ui/opportunities` page is the fastest way to answer that question. Cards combine the current business identity, lifecycle state, Opportunity Score, evidence reasons, recommended offer, confidence, blockers, and a safe next action. Start with a high-signal card, open its linked workspace, and verify the evidence before preparing a proposal or delivery step.

Cards are a UI projection over existing records. They do not modify scoring, approve outreach, or send messages. Suppressed and ambiguous records remain visibly blocked so the operator understands why no action is available.

## Safety boundary

LBOE does not send email, WhatsApp, SMS, or CRM messages. Proposal packs are not contracts, and delivery approvals are operator assertions rather than e-signatures.
