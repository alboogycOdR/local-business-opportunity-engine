# Operator Experience

The operator's primary question is: **where is the next best opportunity, why does it matter, and what should I do next?**

## Opportunity Cards

The `/ui/opportunities` page is the fastest way to answer that question. Cards combine the current business identity, lifecycle state, Opportunity Score, evidence reasons, recommended offer, confidence, blockers, and a safe next action. Start with a high-signal card, open its linked workspace, and verify the evidence before preparing a proposal or delivery step.

Cards are a UI projection over existing records. They do not modify scoring, approve outreach, or send messages. Suppressed and ambiguous records remain visibly blocked so the operator understands why no action is available.

Start at `/ui/opportunities` for the prioritized work queue. Use `/ui` for the
dashboard and campaign map, then open a business workspace to run the explicit
next action: audit, refresh score/brief, prepare a brief, generate a concept
preview, create a proposal pack, or continue delivery. Optional enrichment is
deliberately secondary to the required evidence baseline.

Review Gap Opportunity cards use stored rating and review-count observations, plus conservative comparisons to same-campaign peers. They help an operator decide whether to inspect the evidence; they do not assert ranking changes, customer loss, or revenue outcomes.

## Safety boundary

LBOE does not send email, WhatsApp, SMS, review requests, or CRM messages. Proposal packs are not contracts, and delivery approvals are operator assertions rather than e-signatures.
