# Sprint 27 — First paid client delivery workflow

Sprint 27 adds operator-managed delivery projects after an approved proposal, a won lead, or an explicitly operator-created project. Projects track intake facts, access/assets status without storing credentials, milestones, operator-recorded client approvals, and sanitized local delivery exports.

Important boundaries: credentials belong in an approved password manager; approvals are operator assertions and not e-signatures; exports are local artifacts and are not sent by LBOE. Payments, billing, contracts, e-signature, automated deployment, sending, inbox sync, and CRM sync are out of scope.
