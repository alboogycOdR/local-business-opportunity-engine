# Score calibration guide

Opportunity Score measures addressable opportunity and delivery confidence, not
business quality. Review identity and suppression holds, separate no-website
from healthy-site opportunities, inspect source type/confidence, then compare
pilot distributions. Require observed operator outcomes before changing rules.

Do not use protected attributes, inferred income, copied reviews, or subjective
design language. Any weight change requires before/after fixtures and an
explicit false-positive/false-negative trade-off.
