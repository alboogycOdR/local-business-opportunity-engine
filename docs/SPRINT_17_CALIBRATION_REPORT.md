# Sprint 17 calibration report

Based on the first real Cape Town batch (10 businesses): 5 high, 2 medium,
and 3 low scores; average 67.6. Ten audits and briefs completed. Two demos
were generated, one passed QA, one failed QA, and one was approved. One lead
reached `OUTREACH_READY`; no contact was logged.

Useful signals included verified conversion paths, website health, no-website
status, booking/WhatsApp/click-to-call/contact-form findings, and technical
failures. Weak evidence was visible where listings lacked a website or source
identifier. The score measures addressable digital opportunity and delivery
confidence, not business quality.

Calibration recommendations:

- Keep the QA gate and never share QA-failed demos.
- Require operator evidence review for no-website and source-ID gaps.
- Improve batch observability before changing weights.
- Collect replies and meetings before recalibrating numeric thresholds.
- Keep manual contact outside LBOE and system delivery at zero.
