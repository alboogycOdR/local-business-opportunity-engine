# Commercial operating procedure

Choose a vertical/geography, create a campaign, discover/import, review evidence, audit/score, generate and review a concept demo, prepare an outreach draft, contact manually outside LBOE, record CRM outcomes, generate/review a proposal, create a delivery project, track intake/milestones, export records, and complete a retrospective. No automated sending, copied reviews, unsupported claims, credential storage, contracts/e-signatures, or payment collection.
