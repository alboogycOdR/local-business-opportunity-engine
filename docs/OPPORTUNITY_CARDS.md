# Opportunity Cards

Opportunity Cards are the operator's short list of the next best business actions. They turn existing discovery, audit, score, brief, demo, proposal, and delivery evidence into a scan-friendly card; they do not introduce a new scoring or sales engine.

Each card answers:

- who the business is and where it is located;
- why the evidence makes it an opportunity;
- which offer category fits the current evidence;
- how confident the evidence is; and
- what safe action the operator should take next.

Offer labels are derived conservatively:

- no website → Starter Website;
- conversion findings → Conversion Upgrade;
- technical findings → Technical Cleanup;
- approved demo → review the concept;
- approved proposal → Proposal Follow-up;
- active delivery project → Delivery Follow-up;
- suppression or a do-not-contact hold → No action;
- ambiguous or weak evidence → Review Required;
- otherwise → Score Only or Review Required.

## Review Gap Opportunity

Review Gap cards identify a business whose stored rating or review volume may look weaker than comparable campaign peers. The signal is conservative:

- weak rating: below 4.0 stars;
- very low review volume: fewer than 25 reviews;
- peer rating gap: a same-campaign peer is at least 0.4 stars higher; or
- peer review gap: a same-campaign peer has at least twice the reviews and at least 20 more reviews.

Peers are matched by campaign first, then category and locality when those values are available. If there are not enough peers, the card shows only the business's own review signal. Evidence is phrased as a review-gap signal; LBOE does not claim search-ranking effects, lost customers, or revenue impact.

Review Gap cards lead to **Review evidence**. They do not send review requests, post to Google Business Profile, automate referrals, or contact customers.

Confidence is a display signal based on the stored brief confidence, score band, and safety holds. It is not a new score and does not override the deterministic Opportunity Score.

Use the card to open the existing business, demo, proposal, or delivery workspace. Verify evidence before taking an operator action. LBOE never sends email, WhatsApp, SMS, or CRM messages.
